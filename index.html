How to Use This Demonstrator

Drag the telescope around to see how the three main types of spectra (continuous, absorption, and emission) are obtained from a cold, thin gas cloud and an incandescent lightbulb in space.

The telescope can also be moved from the keyboard: use the Telescope position along the path slider below the space view, or click the telescope once and then use the arrow keys.
